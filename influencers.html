These are a great option for writing and coloring! The clickart pens are my favorite pens to use for mostly anything and everything in my journal. I use them on pretty much every page I do in my journal. There is also a really good variety of colors to choose from and use!

Double-sided tape is an essential for my journal. I have found that it works much better than most glue pens and regular tape inside of a journal. The pictures and images you place inside aren't budging for the most part once you place it down with the double-sided tape. Pretty much all double-sided tape is the same so I try to find cheap options that come in bulk!
